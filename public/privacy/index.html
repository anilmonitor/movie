1 Overview & Purpose

Welcome to Movie Man ("we", "our", or "us"). Movie Man is developed as an informational movie catalog, entertainment guide, release calendar, and cinema exploration tool. Our mobile application allows users to discover movie summaries, release dates, ratings, cast information, category listings, and official promotional trailers.

This Privacy Policy describes our policies and procedures on the collection, use, disclosure, and protection of information when you download, install, or interact with the Movie Man application on your mobile device.

2 Information We Collect

Movie Man is designed to function seamlessly without requiring account registration or user login. We prioritize user privacy and collect only minimal technical information necessary for application stability:

⚙️ Technical & Device Data

General, non-identifying telemetry such as device brand/model, Android OS version, screen resolution, app version, language preference, and general network connection state (Wi-Fi or Cellular).

📊 Performance & Diagnostic Logs

Anonymous crash reports, stack traces, and rendering latency logs provided through standard Google Play developer console diagnostics to identify bugs and enhance stability.

💾 Local On-Device Preferences

Your saved watchlist items, search history queries, and viewing preferences are stored strictly locally on your device via secure device storage and are never transmitted to external servers.

3 Information We Do NOT Collect

To ensure complete compliance with Google Play Store User Data policies and privacy legislation:

  • No Personal Identifiers: We do not ask for or collect your full name, physical address, phone number, or government-issued ID.
  • No Financial or Payment Data: Movie Man does not collect credit card details, banking credentials, or transaction records.
  • No Precise Geolocation: We do not track or request access to your GPS or precise location data.
  • No Contacts or Media Files: We do not request permission to access your personal phonebook contacts, camera, microphone, or private media files.
  • No Biometric Data: No fingerprint, facial recognition, or biometric identification is collected or processed.

4 How We Use Information

The non-identifying information we collect is utilized strictly for the following operational purposes:

  • App Functionality: Delivering accurate movie information, genre listings, release years, and poster previews.
  • Performance Optimization: Monitoring application stability, identifying memory bottlenecks, and resolving software crashes.
  • User Experience: Ensuring the user interface displays properly across diverse screen sizes, orientations, and Android versions.
  • Safety & Integrity: Protecting the application against automated abuse, fraudulent requests, or security threats.

7 Device Permissions & Justifications

Movie Man requests only the minimal essential runtime permissions required to provide core entertainment discovery features:

  • android.permission.INTERNET: Required to download up-to-date movie metadata, synopses, and promotional poster thumbnails from secure endpoints.
  • android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Required to verify whether the mobile device has active Wi-Fi or cellular connectivity before initiating requests.

Movie Man does NOT request dangerous runtime permissions such as camera access, microphone recording, read/write phone contacts, or GPS location.

8 Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Our services are not targeted at or designed for children under 13 years of age (or under 16 in the European Union). We do not knowingly solicit, collect, or process personal information from children. If we discover that a child has provided us with any personal information without verified parental consent, we take immediate steps to delete such information from our records.

If you are a parent or legal guardian and believe that your child has provided us with personal data, please contact us immediately at anilarangi6@gmail.com.

9 Data Security & Retention

We value your trust in using Movie Man. We implement commercially reasonable administrative, technical, and physical security measures to safeguard data:

  • Transport Layer Security (TLS/HTTPS): All API network requests are encrypted in transit via standard HTTPS encryption.
  • Zero Remote Personal Storage: Because Movie Man does not store user profiles on external servers, user personal data is not stored in centralized databases susceptible to breaches.
  • Local Cache Clearing: You can wipe all locally cached movie posters, search history, and saved watchlist items at any time through your device's application manager: Settings > Apps > Movie Man > Storage > Clear Data.

10 User Rights & Data Deletion (GDPR / CCPA)

Depending on your region (including the European Economic Area, United Kingdom, and California), you have specific statutory rights concerning data protection:

  • Right to Know / Access: You may request confirmation on whether any personal data concerning you is processed.
  • Right to Deletion / Erasure: Because we do not maintain accounts or server-side user profiles, uninstalling the application or clearing app cache completely erases all local data.
  • Right to Opt-Out: You may reset your device's Google Advertising Identifier at any time via Google Settings > Ads > Reset Advertising ID.
  • Non-Discrimination: We will not discriminate against any user for exercising their privacy rights.

To submit a formal data deletion inquiry or verification request, email us directly at anilarangi6@gmail.com.
